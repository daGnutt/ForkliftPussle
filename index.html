Forklift Frenzy

Stack the boxes. Park the forklift. Mind the tail swing.

Scoring: each box in a finished zone is worth 100 points, minus up to 50 for crashes and up to 50 for crooked placement (within 5° is free). You need 50% of the maximum to finish. Your final score loses 1 point per 10 seconds.

Tip: you start in the top-down overview. Press Tab/M to swap to the cab view, C for a small corner window.
