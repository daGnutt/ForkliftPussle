Forklift Frenzy

Stack the boxes. Park the forklift. Mind the tail swing.

Tip: you start in the cabin view. Press F to swap to the top-down overview, C for a small corner window.

Press any key to start

How to play

  • Drive the forklift (↑/↓, ←/→) — the rear wheels steer, so the tail swings out. Space brakes.
  • Drive the forks under a box and press A to lift it; Z lowers it (it is released on reaching the floor or a stack).
  • Fill every coloured floor zone with exactly the number of boxes shown, all of that colour.
  • Avoid crashes and place boxes squarely to keep your score up, then park on the marked spot.
  • F swaps cabin / overview view, C shows a small corner window, R restarts, M mutes, H shows this help.
