Pallet Polka is a top-down puzzle game for your browser, played entirely with the keyboard. You drive a forklift around a warehouse and move coloured boxes onto matching coloured zones. A zone counts as done only when it holds a stack of exactly the number of boxes it asks for, all in its colour. The forklift steers with its rear wheels, so the tail swings wide and parking takes real finesse. Forks go from the floor up to five box heights. You can lift whole stacks or just the upper boxes, carry them over other stacks, and set them down on top of each other. Once every zone is filled, you finish by reversing back into your parking spot.

The levels are generated from a seed, so the same seed always gives the same layout. You can share your favourite seeds with friends and compare scores. Each box earns points for being in the right place, but crashes and sloppy, crooked placements cost you, and so does time. Hard mode adds realistic pallet rules: you have to approach square, and the forklift gets slower and top-heavy the higher the forks are. There's also a cab (first-person) view, a polka soundtrack with other MIDI tunes, and a tutorial for newcomers. It needs no installation, runs in Firefox, and has on-screen controls for touch devices.
