Pallet Polka

The development timeline of a top-down, keyboard-only forklift puzzle
21 October – 12 November 2026 · 617 commits · vanilla JS + Canvas 2D · no build, no dependencies
← / → or Space to navigate

The idea & the stack

  • Stack coloured boxes in matching drop zones, then park the forklift
  • A real forklift to drive: it steers with the rear wheels, so the tail swings out
  • Endless supply of generated levels
  • Plain web page: no installs, no libraries, plays on desktop and phone

Day 1 · 21 Oct · Morning

Core mechanics: forks & stacks

  • First playable browser game: forklift, boxes, zones, levels
  • Lift and lower the forks to pick up and stack boxes up to five high
  • Lower a carried stack onto another and it is set down
  • Boxes turn and tumble as you push them
Day 1 · 21 Oct · Midday

Rules, scoring & branding

  • Scoring: crashes and crooked boxes cost points
  • Park the forklift to finish the level
  • Sound: engine, music, fork sounds and a success chime
  • First name, splash screen and 3D-looking boxes
  • Pick any level seed, or roll a random one
Day 1 · 21 Oct · Afternoon & evening

Cabin view, tutorial & high scores

  • First-person cabin view from the driver's seat
  • The view rises and falls with the forks
  • High scores per level, with a time penalty
  • Step-by-step tutorial for new players
Day 3 · 23 Oct

Physics depth & new levels

  • Hard mode: pallets slide, split and get pushed around
  • Badly placed boxes fall off the stack
  • Levels are now rooms and corridors
  • Pause menu with full screen, mute and level select
  • Damage popups and a break sound
Day 4 · 24 Oct

Handling & factory atmosphere

  • Driving with raised forks is slower; steering feels better
  • The top-down overview becomes the default view
  • Floors and walls get markings, windows, doors and signs
  • Ceiling, factory lights and fans
Day 5 · 25 Oct

Sound, saves & touch

  • Reversing beep and light
  • Selectable music and wall bonk and scrape sounds
  • Progress is saved between sessions
  • On-screen touch controls and an installable app
  • First version of this presentation
Days 6–7 · 26 – 27 Oct

On the phone

  • Landscape layout: steering on the left, drive and forks on the right
  • Zoomed overview that follows the truck, so the controls never hide the action
  • Simpler driving: no brake pedal, the engine slows you down
  • More reliable touch steering
Day 8 · 28 Oct

A splash with style

  • Animated splash: a forklift drifting a figure eight, leaving skidmarks
  • Clearer walls and floors; the in-game forklift matches the splash
  • Bigger levels
  • Full screen is now optional
Day 8 · 28 Oct

New soundtrack & calmer menus

  • New music library: country, classical, industrial metal, techno and more, all free to use
  • Music keeps playing in the pause menu
  • Tutorial restart moved into the pause menu
Days 8–9 · 28 – 29 Oct

The cabin view, rebuilt

  • Cabin view rebuilt from scratch
  • New forks; the view tilts with them
  • More factory details: AC units, roller doors and slogan posters
  • Doorways with hazard markings
  • Runs faster
Days 9–10 · 29 – 31 Oct

Look windows & a calmer floor

  • Mirrors replaced by two small look windows: one ahead of the forks, one behind the tail
  • Start stacks gather in marked bays; fewer random floor markings
  • Zone labels are hidden behind walls and boxes
  • Touch tip: tap the minimap to swap views
Day 12 · 1 Nov

Online scores & a testing site

  • High scores are shared online; see your best and the world's best
  • A separate testing version of the game for trying out new features
  • Touch polish: clearer buttons and a better portrait layout
Days 12–13 · 1 – 2 Nov

Wonky stacks & sturdier levels

  • Stacks start slightly crooked; delivering one crooked earns a sad wah-wah
  • Levels no longer have unreachable boxes or zones
  • Saves are more reliable
  • 43 humorous slogan posters for the walls (a few on the next slide)

Disco in the dark

Renamed: Pallet Polka

Day 18 · 7 Nov

True 3D with WebGL2

  • The overview, cabin view and look windows are now real 3D scenes
  • Dark mode headlights, brake and reverse lights, ceiling lights and disco spots

Day 18 · 7 Nov

A new menu, splash & touch schemes

  • Pause menu redesigned with submenus for sound, visuals and levels
  • Splash gets a Disco start button with a dancing man and a short story

Splash and joystick on the phone

Day 19 · 8 Nov

Chase camera & look around

  • Third-person chase camera that follows the truck (C, menu option or double tap)
  • Look around by dragging or with Q/W; zoom slider
  • Walls are cut low in the overview so nothing hides the action
  • A reversing camera in the cabin
Day 19 · 8 Nov

Doorway flair & tidier walls

  • Doorways get hazard stripes, amber beacons and a glowing lamp
  • Doors and signs only where they make sense on the walls
  • Overview walls match the cabin view
Day 21 · 10 Nov

Playtesting at work

  • Took the game to my workplace for a round of playtesting
  • Colleagues played the game under supervision
  • Watching real players showed what is clear and what is confusing
  • Their feedback feeds straight into the next improvements
Day 21 · 10 Nov

Multiplayer: up to four trucks

  • Host a game and friends join with a party code
  • Shared level and score; each truck has its own colour
  • Trucks collide; hand over or split stacks with your forks
  • Driving stays smooth even with a slow connection
  • Player list with kick and ban; other trucks sound quieter with distance
Day 21 · 10 Nov

Honk!

  • Hold H (two fingers on touch) to honk; every truck has its own pitch
  • A label grows the longer you honk: HONK → HOOOOONK → *HOOOOONK*
  • Off-screen honks show at the edge of the view, so you know where to look
Days 21–22 · 10 – 11 Nov

Sparks, smoke & impact physics

  • Sparks, smoke, dust and confetti
  • Tyre squeal when the wheels spin
  • Off-centre hits spin the truck; fast impacts topple boxes onto nearby stacks
  • Other players pop in and out with smoke and stars
  • Compass and a minimap showing all trucks
Days 21–23 · 10 – 12 Nov

Endless mode: lorries & loading bays

  • New Start endless mode: unload arriving lorries and load the delivery trucks
  • Delivery lorries honk and open their gates when the way is clear
  • Numbered bays with waiting times; the pace adapts to how fast you work
  • Running score, autosave and high scores; works in multiplayer
  • New lorry look, with a starry night sky or daytime clouds
