The idea & the stack

  • Stack coloured boxes in matching drop zones, then park the forklift
  • Rear-wheel-steering bicycle model: the tail swings out
  • Deterministic seeded level generator (mulberry32)
  • DOM-free game logic, so the tests run in plain Node
  • Static files on GitHub Pages (and an installable PWA); Firefox is the primary target

Day 1 · 21 Oct · Morning

Core mechanics: forks & stacks

  • First playable browser game: forklift, boxes, zones, levels
  • Fork heights went from half-steps to whole levels 0–5
  • Tines never collide; the body and carried stack do
  • Lowering a carried stack releases it at resting height
  • Boxes rotate with the forklift and remember their rotation
Day 1 · 21 Oct · Midday

Rules, scoring & branding

  • Per-box scoring: crash and squareness deductions (5° allowance)
  • Parking spot; finishing needs parking and ≥ 50% score
  • Procedural audio: engine whine, music, fork sounds, success chime
  • Rebrand to “Forklift Frenzy”: splash screen, favicon, 3D boxes
  • Custom level seeds and a random-seed button
Day 1 · 21 Oct · Afternoon & evening

Cabin view, tutorial & high scores

  • Polygon-projected first-person view with forks, pallets, zones and parking
  • Camera follows fork height; rear-view and side mirrors
  • Nudging a box slowly squares it to the forklift
  • Local high scores per level and seed, with a time penalty
  • Step-by-step, action-dismissed tutorial; pause on lost focus
Day 3 · 23 Oct

Physics depth & new levels

  • Hard mode: pallets glide, split or get pushed; tines hit pallet runners
  • Boxes need 50% support or fall beside the stack; crooked towers
  • Rooms-and-corridors level generator replaces random pillars
  • Escape menu with full screen, mute, go-to-level and shortcuts
  • Floating damage popups and a break sound
Day 4 · 24 Oct

Handling & factory atmosphere

  • Height-based speed limit for raised forks; larger, speed-tapered steering lock
  • Overview becomes the default view and is remembered
  • Floor and wall decals: tire marks, yellow markings, windows, doors, signs
  • Ceiling, factory lights and fans; walls raised to clear a 5-box stack
  • Box decals and a ?debug overlay
Day 5 · 25 Oct

Sound, saves & touch

  • Reversing beep and a blue reversing light that walls cut off
  • Selectable MIDI music (public domain / CC0) with a drum synth; wall bonk and scrape sounds
  • Progress saved in localStorage; export/import of level state
  • Wall-mounted charger to park against; carried stacks can't be lifted into the ceiling
  • On-screen touch controls and an installable PWA
  • First version of this presentation
Days 6–7 · 26 – 27 Oct

On the phone

  • Rotatable landscape layout: field on top, steering bottom left, drive/forks bottom right, minimap and status between
  • Zoomed follow-camera overview; the map pans past its edges so pads never hide it
  • Brake removed (engine braking does the slowing); touch start enters full screen
  • Touch state derived from live touches (fixes flaky PWA steering); service worker bypasses the HTTP cache
Day 8 · 28 Oct

A splash with style

  • Splash: a forklift drifting a figure eight with smoke, boxes and permanent skidmarks
  • Higher wall/floor contrast; the in-game forklift restyled to match the splash
  • Bigger levels now that the map pans
  • Fullscreen is an opt-in menu setting; pause-menu touches no longer reach the pad
  • Service worker auto-reloads on a new version
Day 8 · 28 Oct

New soundtrack & calmer menus

  • Music library replaced: country, classical, industrial metal, techno and more, all CC0 / public domain
  • Music keeps playing in the pause menu and when focus is lost
  • Tutorial restart moved from the H key to a pause-menu button
  • Pause-menu shortcuts for tutorial, resume, auto-fullscreen and hard mode dropped
