2026-10-21 The prototype

First prototype

Top-down forklift on a tiled floor. A rear-wheel-steering bicycle model makes the tail swing out; push coloured boxes onto matching zones.

6974f64

Fork levels

Forks get whole levels 0–5 (A/Z). Boxes stack into towers, tines never collide with boxes, and a gauge shows the fork height.

483ab49

Scoring

Per-box scoring: crashes and crooked placement deduct points; the HUD shows locked damage and fixable placement deductions.

024454d

Parking spot

Finishing needs the truck parked in the marked spot with at least 50% score.

9052fe1

Graphical overhaul

The game gets a name (Forklift Frenzy), a splash screen, a favicon and 3D-looking boxes.

ca4b8d9

Extruded 3D forklift

The truck is drawn as extruded blocks, matching the boxes.

16d0c1e

First cabin view

A first-person POV window renders the world from the driver's seat with a hand-written projection, no WebGL.

157176a

Cabin view grows up

Pallets under every box, forks, drop zones, parking spot and lids on box tops; F swaps big and small views.

06af15d

Dark mode

Colour scheme follows the OS (shown here in light mode).

e5d7189

Tutorial

A step-by-step tutorial where each step disappears when you do it. The cabin view is the default for now.

8ffe630

Mirrors

Rear-view and side mirrors in the cabin view.

3ad7200

Floor grid and walls

Textured floor and walls give the cabin view a sense of speed.

Forks going up and down one level at a time (shown on the day-1 build).

2026-10-23 Realistic pallets and real levels

Hard mode: pallet physics

Tines collide with the runners of pallets and turn them by the push torque; slide in straight or push the pallet away.

b534a9e

Crooked towers

Boxes need 50% support on the box below or fall off; stacks keep individual offsets so towers can be built crooked or toppled.

c26932d

Rooms and corridors

The level generator makes rooms and corridors instead of random pillars.

c328e96

Escape menu

Engine whine, music, hard mode and restart in a pause menu.

a0ae615

Polygon-projection cabin renderer

The cabin view is rewritten as a depth-sorted polygon renderer with smoother tine sorting.

67764be

Damage popups

Floating damage numbers and a break sound when you crash.

a7c6dbb
