# State, rules, scoring, level generation, rendering

## Units and coordinates
- Simulation units are tiles. Crates and pallets are 1×1 (wide: 1×2). Drop points are 15% wider and deeper than their original markings: 1.38×1.38 (wide: 1.38×2.53, or the swapped orientation). Barrel pallets use the same footprint and have a taller deck to clear the tines in 3D without changing logical fork levels. `geom.js` shares footprint, pallet-runner dimensions and `zoneExt` between physics, generation and renderers (`game.js` re-exports `zoneExt`).
- x right, y down; angle `a` in radians, 0 = facing +x.
- Forklift local frame: x forward, y right, origin at the **front** axle; rear wheels steer. `toWorld`/`toLocal` convert.
- Floor stacks always start at logical height 0. `stackZ(st)` reads only the drawing-time `fallZ` from `fallView`, never a floor elevation; collision and fork rules use `g.H` directly.

## Game state `g` (from `newGame`)
- `walls`: border + interior wall rects with a `poly` (collision); in endless mode the south/west/east border walls carry `hidden: true` (solid, but the GL scene doesn't draw them) and the north one has `draw` (a thin strip clipped to the warehouse's width that the GL scene draws instead). `pillars`: the raw interior wall rects (for drawing).
- `doors`: `{x,y,w,h}` doorway gaps in the dividing walls (cosmetic; decals only). - `zones`: `{x, y, color, count}` targets. `areas`: `{color, x, y, w, h}` tinted regions; all zones of a colour sit in one area (wide zones get an area of their own). `bays`: `{x, y, w, h}` start-stack clusters: stacks are grouped 1-4 per bay on a 2-tile grid, bays kept ≥1 from walls and doorways, ≥1.5 from drop-off areas/each other; drawn as a big yellow outline floor decal (`floorDecals`), which also keeps random yellow decals out of bays.
- `stacks`: floor stacks `{x, y, layers, a}`; `layers` are colour indices into `COLORS` (bottom → top); `rots` is a parallel array with each box's own world angle (remembered through lift/drop/merge); `a` is the bottom box's angle, used for collision. Fresh stacks start slightly wonky (`wonkyStack` in `game.js`: deterministic per position, each box turned up to 15° and shifted up to 0.06 tiles; `a` = `rots[0]`); `offs` is a parallel array of each box's world offset `{x, y}` from the stack position (`offs[0]` = 0), so towers can be crooked; `pen` is a parallel array of `{crash, sq, deco}` per box (point deductions and decal kind), with `inspected: true` once photographed, saved and synced with the box. Oil barrels use a matching two-layer pair (`barrelPart: 'base'|'top'`, shared `barrelId`) to occupy two box heights while remaining one rigid barrel on the bottom pallet.
- `oil`: persistent floor puddles `{id, x, y, r, drops}` from damaged barrels, saved and synced to multiplayer. Each damage point (sum of the barrel pair's crash points) leaks one drop per ten seconds. The base `pen` stores `oilDropped` (0..50) and `oilFraction` (fractional progress toward the next drop), so lifting, saving and multiplayer preserve the remaining supply. Each drop adds area `1.15² / 50` in radius-squared units; overlapping puddles merge with area-weighted centres and conserved area, so 50 drops give the original 1.15-tile-radius spill. Spills stay where they fall; the finite supply replaces the old 256-spill cap. Front-wheel contact reduces drive traction, while rear-wheel contact reduces steering grip and can add yaw and sideways drift.
- `inspector` (dynamic; `null` without a hatch) `{x, y, a, show, mode, shooting, flash, roll (wheel angle, rad, from distance driven), scared, path, repath, target, seen: [zoneKey]}`, saved and synced like `bot`; drawn in all three views.
- `hatch` (static, `null` without barrels or drop points) `{x, y, nx, ny, a}` on a wall and `bot` (dynamic; `null` without a hatch) `{x, y, a, show 0..1, mode 'hidden'|'out', cleaning, scared, path, repath, fleePath}`: see `js/bot.js`. `bot` is saved (`DYNAMIC`) and synced in multiplayer snapshots; drawn in the 2D, GL and software cabin views (hatch always, bot while `show > 0`). `show` is the progress of sliding out of / into the hatch at full size (`botDrawPos(b, hatch)` gives the drawn position, up to `BOT_SLIDE` back inside the wall, which hides the bot: the 2D view clips bots to the map rectangle, GL/software views rely on the wall geometry); no scaling. Both ways are one straight line square to the wall: an emerging bot drives from `botHome` out to the lane point `botLane` (`LANE` from the wall) facing out, `show` being its progress along that line; the home goal of both bots is the lane point, where the bot turns to face the wall and then drives straight in (forwards both ways) (`emerge`/`retract` in `bot.js`; `bot.leaving` marks a retract in progress, which is always finished). Forklifts do not collide with it; it keeps out of their way.
- `fl`: forklift `{x, y, a, speed, steer}`.
- `carried`: array of layers being carried, or `null`; `carriedRots`: each carried box's angle relative to the forklift (so they turn with it, and keep their world angle when dropped); `carriedOffs`: each carried box's offset from the fork carry point in the forklift's frame, including the bottom box (`carriedWorldOffs(g)` rotates it to world). Lifts and handoffs preserve each box's horizontal position. `carriedRel` = `carriedRots[0]`, used for collision. `carriedPen`: the carried boxes' `pen` entries.
- `H`: logical fork level (integer 0–5); `hf`: smoothed value used only for drawing.
- `parking`: `{x, y, a}` centre/heading of the parking spot (the forklift's spawn pose; random per level, axis-aligned heading, tail resting against a charger: a half-truck-width stub added to `walls`/`pillars` jutting 0.45 from a random outer wall, generated in `level.js` `tryGenerate`; also returned as `charger`/`g.charger`, flagged `charger: true` in `g.walls`, drawn red with high-voltage decals: `drawCharger` in `render.js`, a 0.9-high (1.5 boxes) prism in `pov.js` with `drawChargerDecal`). When the spot turns green it also shows an arrow for the required heading (`render.js`, `pov.js`).
- `hits`: damage popups `{x, y, z, pts, t}` (z in box heights, `t` = `g.clock` at creation), created by `hurt()` in `game.js` whenever crash points are actually added and dropped after `HIT_LIFE` (1.2 s); `hitCount` increases per popup (`main.js` plays `playBreak` when it rises). Crash popups are drawn as floating red `−pts` text in the overview (`drawOverview`) and the big cabin view (`drawHits` in `pov.js`). Score popups (`scorePopups`, end of `update`; per-zone `zonePoints` of the previous step in `g.zonePts`; zones already complete at the first update stay quiet) are hits `{x, y, z, t, owner: null, zone, score}` in two independent kinds: `score` = `{total}` (points before wonkiness, `base` of `zonePoints(g, z)` = `{n, base, total, crash, square}`: 100 per box, crash damage is not deducted since the live score already has it; shown when a drop point is completed, negative when the stack is moved off it) and `{wonky}` (signed score change from the crooked placement or from nudging the stack more or less crooked; a popup still showing for the zone is merged into, refreshed, and dropped when the change cancels out). So the stack score can fade while nudging keeps re-popping the wonkiness. They live `SCORE_LIFE` (2.2 s, `hitLife(h)`), don't bump `hitCount`, and are drawn like damage popups via `hitRows`/`labelRows` in `pov/overlay.js`: `Score: ±N` (green, red when negative) and, one row lower, `Wonkiness: ±N` (amber when it costs, green when it gains); damage has its own red popup when it happens.
- `status`: `'play'` or `'won'`; `time`: level timer in seconds, starts when the truck first moves (`started`), stops once `status` is `'won'` and restarts at 0 with the next `newGame`; `clock`: always-running animation clock (hit popups, blink).

## Rules (see `game.js`)
- Fork level `H` = box heights above the floor. A stack of n boxes spans heights 0..n. `forkUp` lifts the box at height `H` and everything above it; level 0 lifts the whole stack.
- Raised forks are top-heavy and slow the forklift down: top speed and acceleration scale by `heightSpeedFactor(g.H)` (`forklift.js`, linear from 1 at `H=0` to `MIN_SPEED_FACTOR` at `H=MAX_H`); exceeding the new cap after raising the forks bleeds off like rolling resistance rather than snapping instantly. Braking is unaffected by height.
- **Hard mode** (`g.hard`, splash `#hardOpt`, persisted as `forklift-hard`; toggling restarts the level) enables pallet collisions, tine torque, gliding, split damage, and inspector-only placement feedback. Off by default: forks lift from any angle and tines never collide.
- In hard mode, floor stacks ignore crash impacts below 1.5 tiles/s; harder impacts deal 25% less damage, rounded to whole points with a minimum of 1 per impact.
- There is no entry-angle rule: forks only get into a pallet because the tines physically fit its slots (below); `forkUp` lifts the stack whose box at the fork level is in the fork zone (`forkedStack`). A pallet on the tines glides with them (`glideWithForks`): while the forklift turns it stays fixed sideways relative to the forks and turns with them, but keeps its world position along the fork axis, so driving in/out slides the forks through it. The whole stack moves, but only boxes from the fork level upward (`rots[i]`, i ≥ `H`) turn; `a` (bottom box) only changes at level 0. Not lifted.
- A tine push that damages a stack affects only the box at fork height; boxes above and below it are not damaged by that contact. In hard mode, if a shifted upper box loses support while both tines are beneath it, it is caught and carried when the load fits under the ceiling; other unsupported boxes fall normally.
- Tines collide with the pallet's *runners* (`palletRunners`: a centre runner and two outer boards along the box's local x axis, i.e. perpendicular to its tape strip, leaving a slot per tine) of any stack taller than `H`; coming in along that axis (either end) they slide into the slots, from the sides they hit the runners. The cabin view pallets (`pov/scene.js` `addStack`) and the 2D top view tape strip match the GL pallet: three runners plus five deck slats. A tine pushing off-centre turns the pallet by the lever arm of the push (`spinStack`, boxes from the fork level upward, clamped per contact); the truck body, a carried stack (when moving ≥ `CRASH_MIN_SPEED`) and stack-on-stack contact turn stacks the same way.
- Otherwise tines never collide with boxes. Only the forklift body and the carried stack (when `stack.layers.length > g.H`) do.
- Empty `forkDown` with a box under the forks (`stack.layers.length >= H`, `H > 0`) is refused: returns `'blocked'`, adds 10 crash points (`FORK_BLOCK_POINTS`) to that box; `main.js` then plays `playBlocked`.
- `forkDown` releases the carried stack automatically at its resting height (top of the stack below, or the floor), merging into it at the position it was released (keeping its offset, so towers can be crooked). `settle()` (also run after a box is pushed) makes any box with less than `MIN_SUPPORT` (50%, `overlapArea`) of its area on the box below fall, with everything above it, 1.45 tiles beside the stack as a new stack with maximum crash damage. Carried boxes collide with and push the boxes of a stack at level ≥ `H` individually (`shiftBoxes`; pushing box 0 moves the whole stack), tines push the box at level `H` and above; boxes above the fork level that slid more than `SLID_MIN` (0.2) off the box below (`slidBoxes`) are hit by the body/fork plate (only within `BODY_REACH` = 3 boxes of the fork level; higher ones need the forks raised) in any mode but never by the tines (which pass through boxes unless hard), so they can be pushed back into line; the body and stack-vs-stack collisions still use the bottom box only, while wall collisions check every box. Max 5 layers (`MAX_LAYERS`). Ceiling: `forkUp` with a carried stack refuses ('blocked', `main.js` plays `playBlocked`, plus `playBreak` via `hitCount`) when `H + carried.length >= CEILING_LEVELS` (6) and gives every carried box `CEILING_POINTS` (10) crash points.
- `score(g)` is the live score: points of zone-completing stacks (100 per box minus crash and crooked placement) minus crash damage on loose stacks and carried boxes (so it can be negative, and is not deducted again on drop). A zone is done only when a stack of exactly `zone.count` boxes, all of `zone.color`, is within 0.5 of it. Winning (`isFinished`) = all zones done (`isSolved`) AND forklift parked (no minimum score) (`isParked`: within 0.6 of the spot, heading within 0.35 rad, speed < 0.5).
- Nudging: while driving forward slowly (0.05 ≤ speed < `CRASH_MIN_SPEED`), contact with the forklift front/mast straightens box levels 0 through `H + 1`. Tines never automatically square a stack: in hard mode they physically push boxes from level `H` upward and rotate them by the contact's lever arm. Contact below `CRASH_MIN_SPEED` does not damage; only contact at or above it counts as a crash.
- Wall alignment: a floor stack pressed into a wall below `CRASH_MIN_SPEED` is collected in `g.wallAligning` and turned square to the (axis-aligned) wall by `alignStack(g, s, dt, 0)`; at or above it the wall snags a corner and `spinStack` turns it (the push-out also swings it sideways). A carried stack (or the tines holding it) touching a wall does the same on the forks: `g.carriedAligning` squares `carriedRel`/`carriedRots` to the wall gently, `turnCarried` twists them when hard, both clamped to `CARRIED_TURN_MAX` from square to the forks.
- Wall sliding: `resolveTruckWalls` and `resolveStackWalls` check each box's own footprint, rotation and offset. A carried upper box slides with the boxes above it along the wall's push-out normal; bottom-pallet contact slides the entire load backward along the fork axis. `slideCarried` limits movement by support on the box below (`MIN_SUPPORT`), overlap with both tines, mast clearance and existing walls/stacks/trucks; unused push-out moves the forklift instead. Barrel pairs move together. Floor upper-box contacts shift that box and above, or the whole stack if that shift would remove support. Carried rotation also preserves support and mast clearance. Existing invalid support/clearance is not made worse by sliding or turning. Below `CRASH_MIN_SPEED` (0.8 tiles/s), wall correction adds no damage or dents; faster contacts keep existing crash rules. `clearWallOverlaps` runs a damage-free cleanup after alignment, and again after multiplayer truck steps (another truck's horn/push can move an already-stepped load), so rotation and honks cannot leave boxes inside walls. Offsets persist through the existing save, sync, lift and drop fields; no new state is needed.
- Honk shockwave: on a horn key rising edge (0.4 s cooldown, `truck.waveAt`) `hornWave()` in `game.js` jump-scares every stack within `WAVE_RADIUS` (3.5) of the truck's nose, and every truck's carried stack in range: `wiggleBoxes` really shifts (≤ 0.05) and turns (≤ 0.06 rad) boxes 1.. (the floor stack's bottom box only ≤ 0.03; the carried bottom box stays square to the forks), keeping each box within `WIGGLE_MAX_OFF`/`WIGGLE_MAX_TURN` of the one below and ≥ `WIGGLE_SUPPORT` (80%) supported, else the stack is left unchanged, so a honk never topples anything. Also stamps `stack.jolt` / `truck.jolt` (= `g.clock`): a drawing-only 0.35 s hop (`joltHop`, via `fallView`'s `fallZ` for floor stacks and `carriedJoltZ` added to the carried height in `render.js`, `gl/models.js`, `pov/scene.js`). `jolt` is transient (not saved or synced).
- **Oil barrels**: from level 3, two matching box layers are converted to `BARREL_COLOR` and moved to the top of their stack, with a separate one-barrel zone added (generator maximum rises to four). They are drawn as one drum on one pallet; forks lift it only from its base, the two layers stay rigid, and no load can be stacked on top. Each damage point leaks one drop per ten seconds from a 50-drop supply; nearby drops join into larger spills, conserving oil area. Oil affects axles independently: front-wheel contact reduces drive traction; rear-wheel contact reduces steering grip and can make the truck yaw and drift.
- `resolve()` runs iterative SAT: forklift shapes vs walls; forklift shapes vs stacks (mass-weighted pushing); stacks vs walls; stacks vs stacks.

## Scoring (`game.js`)
- Each box is worth 100 points, but only boxes in a stack that completes a zone count towards `score(g)`; score = sum of (100 − crash − square) over those boxes, so it starts at 0 on every `newGame` and rises as zones are filled; `maxScore` is 100 × all boxes.
- Crash (up to 50 per box): forklift body or carried boxes hitting a floor stack, stack-vs-stack contact, or carried boxes hitting a wall, at forklift speed ≥ 0.8 (1–5 points, 0.4 s cooldown per stack/carried group).
- Placement (up to 50 per box): `refreshPlacement` computes `pen.sq` from rotation away from the nearest multiple of 90° (free within 5°, then floored in steps of 5 points). Hard mode adds `pen.shift` only when a corner of that box extends beyond the drop point's world-axis rectangle; touching the boundary is allowed. All boxes are measured against the drop point, not the box beneath them. Penalties scale with maximum corner overhang (steps of 5, 50 at 0.35 tiles; no separate offset for a barrel top). Rotation penalties remain independent of containment. The inspector photo snapshot saves both and labels rotated and shifted boxes separately. In Hard mode there is no immediate wonkiness popup or sound, and the HUD shows placement deductions only once a current photo exists; final scores include unreported deductions. Saves use inspection version 2; older Hard-mode reports and badges are discarded for reinspection, but the level and box positions are preserved.
- `lostBreakdown(g)` reports all crash damage split by repair eligibility and `square` (placement lost only on completed drop points). `score`, `zonePoints` and `lostBreakdown` default to full deductions; pass `true` for the live, report-gated values.

## Level generation (`level.js`)
- Deterministic per (level number, seed) via `mulberry32`; `generateLevel(n, seed = 0)`, seed 0 = the default layouts. `parseSeed(text)` maps splash input to an int (integers as-is, other text hashed). `newGame(n, seed)` stores `g.seed`; retries up to 5000 deterministic attempts for that same level number and throws if none is valid (no level-1 fallback).
- Order: choose boxes and zone counts per colour → rooms (`buildRooms`: recursive splits into rooms/corridors by thickness-1 dividing walls, each with one 3.6-wide door, or two (40% chance when the wall is long enough, ≥1.5 of wall between them); later walls keep clear of earlier doors so everything stays connected; `levelParams.dividers`, 0 on level 1) → per-colour zone area (preferably flush against an outer wall, 0.5 gap); its drop points are laid out on the grid with the tallest (`count`) furthest back, towards the outer wall nearest the area (`fillArea`); after the barrel/wide swaps `tidyAreas` shrinks each colour area to its remaining drop points and pushes it flush to that wall → stack placement avoiding areas, walls and the start.
- `isReachable(lvl)` (flood fill on a 0.25 grid from the start, cells within 0.8 of walls/charger/edge blocked) rejects a level (`tryGenerate` returns null → next attempt) unless every stack and zone has a reachable cell within 2.0. Wide stacks and zones also need a reachable straight corridor along the pallet axis, long enough for the truck to withdraw its tines; wide zones must retain this clearance even with the other drop points filled. `tryWide` checks these corridors during placement and may fall back to fewer or no wide zones. Guarantees valid placement, matching box/zone counts and position reachability, **not** full solvability (ignores turning radius/tail swing and movable starting stacks). Difficulty scales in `levelParams` (map width caps at 44 tiles, height 0.7× that; up to 40 boxes and 10 dividers; larger maps rely on the follow camera).
- Generated layouts changed with the wide-corridor validation and barrel-zone update: `savestate.js` records `layoutVersion` 4 and rejects older level-3-and-up saves instead of mixing their boxes with new drop points. Unversioned level 1–2 saves still load; rejected autosaves restart the level.
- After layout selection, colour areas are fitted to the enlarged drop-point bounds without moving any drop point or changing the seeded layout.

## Rendering (`render.js`)
- Height is faked with a parallax offset (`PX`/`PY`). Draw order: floor/grid → areas → zones → low stacks → forklift body → tines → high stacks → carried stack → pillars → HUD.
- The minimap alternates high-contrast colours on open drop points compatible with a single-colour, single-type carried load; mixed loads and already-filled zones are not highlighted. Oil-barrel targets also require an intact barrel pair.
- GL set-down dust (`js/gl/particles.js`) is emitted only when the landing point is outside every drop-zone rectangle; completed zones still emit confetti.
- The forklift is drawn from extruded `block(ctx, f, x0, y0, x1, y1, z0, z1, col)` calls in its local frame (wheels, chassis, counterweight, mast, overhead guard) using the same parallax as boxes.
- `drawStack(ctx, layers, x, y, z0, rots)` draws boxes as extruded cubes (shaded side faces facing the floor-ward parallax direction, gradient lid with tape strip), each with its own angle from `rots`; the top box shows its colour and the boxes below appear as coloured numbers. Open-top boxes have a dark-gray fork-entry tape strap across the opening, rotated with the box; their warning decal appears on top and only on the two fork-entry faces.
- The fork-height gauge shows each box under the forks and each carried box. After inspection, a yellow/red `!` marks repairable damage and a tilted gold diamond marks crooked boxes; badges follow inspected boxes through lifts, splits and saves, but a nudge or collision makes the affected stack forget them until reinspection. They use the same `boxStatus(pen)` rules as the inspector photo and remain visible with Battery saver on.
- Barrel layers render as one two-box-high drum with a single pallet. Oil slicks are drawn as dynamic floor marks in the 2D and GL views and as floor faces in the software cabin view.
- The repair-station hint arrow (`repairhint.js`) scales its outline with its size, keeping the blue fill visible in both tile-unit software overview drawing and pixel-unit GL/cabin overlays.
